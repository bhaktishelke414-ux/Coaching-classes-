ABOUT US

A coaching class run like a classroom, not a factory

We started in 2012 with one rented room and 14 students preparing for their Class 10 boards. Today we run 38 batches across Math, Science, English and Social Studies for Classes 8 to 12, still with the same rule: no batch bigger than 20 students. Our teachers are full-time — not visiting faculty — so they know every student's report card by heart.
